# Blueprint month · complete narration and alternate hooks

Exact canonical narration. Alternates are unscheduled. Research and audience validation are different; comprehension answers below are scoring rubrics, not observed results.

## BP01 · November 1 · First gym visit—what do you do first?

Q opening · planning · 69 words.

**Hook:** First gym visit. What do you do first?

**Answer:** Make three decisions before you walk in.

**Example:** Choose your training days, bring a beginner routine, and know who can show you the equipment.

**Boundary:** An exercise video cannot check your setup in person.

**Action:** Ask the gym about an orientation. Put the routine in your phone so you can follow it there.

**Close:** What feels less clear right now: the plan, or the machines?

**Unscheduled alternative:** Make three decisions before your first gym visit.

**Posting caption:** Before your first visit: choose days, bring a beginner routine and ask about an equipment orientation.

**Comprehension:** Prepare days, routine and setup help; seek an orientation.

**Critical misunderstanding:** A short video checks my individual technique or provides a full routine.

**Example:** Adult research finds muscle growth with both machines and free weights. Strength also depends on what you practice.

**Boundary:** That does not make every exercise interchangeable.

**Action:** Ask for the setup, choose an option in your routine, and record its settings.

**Close:** Which machine would you like explained?

**Unscheduled alternative:** Do you have to start with free weights?

**Posting caption:** Machines are a valid option. Ask about setup and keep the settings in your log; this is not a claim that all exercises are interchangeable.

**Comprehension:** Machines can be useful; specific strength and setup matter.

**Critical misunderstanding:** Every machine and free-weight exercise is identical or equally safe for everyone.
