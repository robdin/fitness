## M15 — Three questions before you change your workout

2026-11-26 · evidence · 71 words

Before a new study rewrites your workout, ask three questions. Who was studied? Their experience and situation might differ from yours. What was compared? The actual programs matter more than the headline labels. What changed, and over how long? Strength and muscle size are different outcomes. These questions do not replace a full appraisal. They help you avoid a rushed conclusion. Save the questions: who, compared with what, and which outcome?
