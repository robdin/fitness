## BK02 · November 11 · Read your workout
1. Learn the language before chasing a number.
2. A rep is one movement. A set is a group of reps.
3. 3 × 8 is three groups of eight, with rest as directed.
4. Starting loads are individual. Ask for setup help; teens need appropriate supervision.
5. Record your work. Soreness is not the score.

A practical checklist from this week’s videos. Record your work. Soreness is not the score.

## BK03 · November 18 · Make the log useful

3. Record settings, load and repetitions.
4. Use comparable rest and stopping rules when comparing.
5. One session is not a growth test. Review the pattern.
