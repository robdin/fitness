# Body of Our Dreams working rules

Read `brand/blueprint/VOICE_DIRECTION.md` before writing public copy.

- Write like an informed friend talking to one person. Use ordinary words, concrete situations and contractions where they sound natural.
- Do not use em dashes in original scripts, captions, profile copy, on-screen text or replies. Rewrite the sentence; do not substitute another decorative dash.
- Avoid stock motivational language, corporate phrasing, forced slang and repeated rhetorical templates. Do not invent personal experience, results or credentials.
- Keep sources, uncertainty and qualifications that change the advice. A natural voice is not a reason to make a stronger claim.
- Do not promise that writing will pass an AI detector or call a style check proof of human authorship. Keep applicable platform disclosures accurate.
- Run `python3 tools/brand/check_copy.py` before building or handing off public copy. The automated checks catch specific patterns; still review the writing for meaning and tone.
- Preserve unrelated work and existing media. Regenerate only affected assets, and never mark live changes complete without observing the result.
