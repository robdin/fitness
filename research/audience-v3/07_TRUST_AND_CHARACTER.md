# Trust, aspiration and character choice

Research addendum · 22 October 2026. Training Club remains selected. Character appearance is an open production hypothesis; no character test or audience recruitment has occurred. This targeted review does not establish a universally best physique for male beginners aged 16–35.

## 1. Does citing evidence earn trust?

**It can, but the effect is contextual. Keep citations for accountability and test their presentation.** Perceived truth, confidence in a presenter, actual accuracy and durable trust are different outcomes.

Putnam and Phelps tested citations in six trivia experiments. Their combined estimate showed a small increase in truth judgments, d = 0.13 (95% CI 0.06–0.20). The materials included true and false claims: a citation can make something seem credible without making it correct. This was text-based trivia, not fitness video or longitudinal brand trust. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28780441/)

More directly relevant to the format, Heiss and colleagues randomized 956 people across approximately 30-second mental-health videos and a control. Adding a scientific reference did not improve the measured outcomes; scientific references did not increase content credibility. This is one topic and presentation, not evidence that all citations are ineffective. The publisher's correction concerns an author's surname, not these findings. [Study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10410236.2023.2301201), [correction](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10410236.2024.2324488)

Our editorial recommendation is a short, readable evidence label beside the supported claim, with the complete reference and scope in the caption/description. For B03, a candidate label is “Schoenfeld et al., 2016 · 21 trained men.” Keep the existing spoken qualification that the result does not establish a universal rest rule. A paper screenshot or “science says” badge alone is weaker accountability. Avoid turning the opening into a bibliography; retain the useful answer and readable captions. This recommendation is a design judgment to test, not a demonstrated retention improvement.

Keep links in both arms of a later citation-placement test. Compare references in the description alone against the same references plus one on-screen label. This isolates the added visual cue more closely than changing the narrator, claims, qualifications and citation together. Measure credibility, comprehension, source recall and viewing separately. A credibility gain accompanied by overgeneralization is not a success.

## 2. Is a muscular character better than a similar-looking one?

**The aspiration hypothesis is plausible, but “more muscular wins” is not validated.** A character can be aspirational and relatable at the same time. Muscularity, overall attractiveness, similarity, competence and attainability should not be treated as interchangeable.

There is positive evidence: a study of 1,428 male Instagram users in Taiwan manipulated comparison direction and found stronger workout-related self-improvement motives with upward comparison to attractive models. Its institutional abstract supports an aspiration mechanism, not a proven increase in attendance, follows or retention for an animated character. [Peng et al., 2019](https://scholar.nycu.edu.tw/en/publications/comparing-and-modeling-via-social-media-the-social-influences-of-/)

There is counterevidence: Edelblum and colleagues found lower liking/following intentions for highly versus moderately attractive fitness influencers, associated with lower relatability. Main studies featured female influencers; the male-influencer follow-up had smaller, directionally similar effects. These were mock posts and stated intentions, not observed social-feed growth. Attractiveness also combines more than muscle size. [Edelblum et al., 2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mar.70023)

In a seven-day experiment with 223 men, repeated fitspiration exposure increased body dissatisfaction compared with neutral images. That makes confidence and discouragement relevant outcomes alongside attention. It does not establish that a clothed, friendly illustrated guide will have the same effect. [Yee et al., 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1740144520303879)

Similarity also has a plausible mechanism: Stanford experiments found exercise-related benefits from self-resembling virtual models. These were immersive, personalized virtual representations, not a generic character on a phone. A less muscular stock character is not automatically “someone like me.” [Fox and Bailenson, 2009](https://vhil.stanford.edu/publications/medical/virtual-self-modeling-effects-vicarious-reinforcement-and-identification)

Finally, a randomized study of 655 college students measured both immediate responses and subsequent fitness-center attendance. Its main predicted benefits of alternative fitspiration messages were not supported. This reinforces the need to distinguish feeling motivated from doing something. It did not isolate character muscularity. [Arigo et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8320884/)

## Production recommendation

My preferred candidate is a **visibly muscular, approachable fictional guide**, fully clothed, with a relaxed expression and a practical role in explaining the graphic. Make competence visible through the explanation. Do not present a physique as proof that the character's advice is correct, label a drawing “naturally achievable,” or invent a personal transformation history for DEX.

Test that candidate against the same character with less muscular definition, plus the existing graphics-only format. Both character versions get the same friendly behavior and useful script. Calling one “aspirational” and the other “relatable” in participant instructions would prime the answer; those are outcomes to measure.

A coach-and-beginner pair could combine aspiration with identification, but it adds a character, dialogue and production work. Reserve it for a later test if the single-character results identify a specific unmet need. Do not combine it with the first physique comparison.
