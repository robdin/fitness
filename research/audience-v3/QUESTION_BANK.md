# 64-question beginner research bank

Original editorial questions informed by source families. These are not quotations, interview findings or 64 independent demand observations. The source family supports a theme; it does not prove demand for every wording. Demographic and access limits are in sources.json.

## A five-question interview that can disprove our assumptions

1. Think of the last fitness question you actually searched: what was it?
2. Where did you start, and what did you open next?
3. What answer changed what you did, if anything?
4. What still felt unclear after watching?
5. Show us what you would look for before your next session, without revealing private account or health information.

Record actual behavior separately from what someone says they might do. Use adult beginners for the initial screen; no outreach has been conducted. Do not ask participants to upload body photos, disclose diagnoses or provide sensitive measurements.
