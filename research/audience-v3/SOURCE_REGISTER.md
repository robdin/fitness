# Research source register

Checked 22 October 2026. Access and scope limits accompany every finding.

## P01 · Huang et al. 2017: question asking and liking

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28447835/) · psychology

Live conversations linked question asking, particularly follow-up questions, with perceived responsiveness and liking.

**Limit:** Dyadic conversations and speed dating are not social-feed comment experiments. Read with P02; do not infer a Reels lift.

**Application:** Ask a specific question and respond to the actual answer.

**Access:** Indexed primary abstract; direct page intermittently returned a stub.

## P02 · 2025 correction to Huang et al.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40111841/) · correction

An independent audit identified minor reporting errors; the correction reports that the original conclusions remain supported.

**Limit:** A correction does not remove limits on transferring results to another context.

**Application:** Keep the correction attached to P01.

**Access:** Indexed correction abstract; DOI 10.1037/pspi0000491.

## P03 · Berger and Milkman 2012: What Makes Online Content Viral?

[Source](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1509/jmr.10.0353) · psychology

The research relates sharing to emotion, arousal and practical usefulness, using news content and experiments.

**Limit:** Email sharing of news is not Shorts distribution; selection, audience and platform differ.

**Application:** Create useful surprise and relief; do not engineer shame or panic.

**Access:** Primary paper search extract and author discussion; no claim of a new systematic review.

## P04 · Gruber et al. 2014: curiosity and memory

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25284006/) · psychology

Controlled trivia research linked higher curiosity with improved memory.

**Limit:** Laboratory memory and neural activity do not establish feed retention or a dopamine editing formula.

**Application:** Use a small answerable gap; assess recall separately from watch time.

**Access:** Indexed primary abstract; direct PubMed page intermittently unavailable.

## P05 · Teixeira et al. 2012: exercise and self-determination

[Source](https://doi.org/10.1186/1479-5868-9-78) · review

The review supports relationships between autonomous motivation, competence and exercise participation.

**Limit:** Adult exercise studies, mixed designs; not evidence that a palette or caption causes adherence.

**Application:** Give achievable choices and explanations; measure whether viewers know what to do next.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## P06 · Le Quéré and Matias 2025: When curiosity gaps backfire

[Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-81575-9) · registered_report

An analysis of 8,977 headline experiments found context-dependent effects of concreteness: both excessive vagueness and excessive detail can be unhelpful.

**Limit:** News click-through outcomes; linguistic feature analyses do not identify a universal fitness hook formula.

**Application:** Name the problem and withhold only the useful detail, then deliver it promptly.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## P07 · Robertson et al. 2023: Negativity drives online news consumption

[Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01538-4) · registered_report

Negative headline language increased news clicks in the studied archive.

**Limit:** Clicks are not trust, learning, health or long-term channel value.

**Application:** Treat fear-led hooks as a tradeoff to reject here, not a growth requirement.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## P08 · Shulman et al. 2024: Reading dies in complexity

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11152133/) · writing_research

More than 30,000 field experiments and a follow-up study support simpler news headlines for general readers.

**Limit:** News headlines; professional writers behaved differently. No promised percentage lift for our videos.

**Application:** Use common words, concrete nouns and short spoken clauses.

**Access:** Indexed paper methods/abstract and author institution summary; PMC direct access challenged.

## P09 · Noetel et al. 2022: Multimedia Design for Learning

[Source](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211052329) · review_of_reviews

A synthesis of multimedia research supports principles including signaling, coherence and spatial contiguity.

**Limit:** Learning experiments are not proof that more animation makes a Short viral.

**Application:** Animate the explanation; keep labels close to the object and remove decorative load.

**Access:** Journal abstract via ERIC EJ1338120 and previous project research.

## P10 · 2020 experiment: fitspiration and men’s body image

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32977202/) · experiment

223 men received randomized image conditions during repeated smartphone assessments; fitspiration exposure increased body dissatisfaction relative to neutral images.

**Limit:** Image exposure and sampled men; not every fitness video or every 16–35-year-old.

**Application:** Show competence, equipment and logs; avoid idealized-body comparison as the engine of engagement.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y01 · YouTube: Search and discovery tips for Shorts

[Source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en) · platform

YouTube describes personalization, choosing to watch, viewing duration/percentage and satisfaction signals. It specifies no minimum posting cadence.

**Limit:** Official qualitative guidance, not published algorithm weights or success thresholds.

**Application:** Four weekly posts fit capacity; diagnose attention and satisfaction separately.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y02 · YouTube: Understand content performance

[Source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en) · platform

Current documentation distinguishes play-start views, engaged views excluding loops, stayed-to-watch, and engaged-view-based duration metrics.

**Limit:** Definitions can change. Preserve metric names, denominator, date window and export date.

**Application:** Use 168-hour snapshots and engaged views; never pool Reels views with Shorts views.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y03 · Meta: Trial Reels

[Source](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/) · platform

Trial Reels initially reach non-followers, with insights around 24 hours and optional automatic sharing based on the first 72 hours.

**Limit:** Some followers can encounter them; this is not randomized A/B assignment. Verify account availability.

**Application:** Optional separate exploratory test; leave auto-sharing off and do not mix trials with the core series.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y04 · Meta: Best practices hub for creators

[Source](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/) · platform

Instagram offers creation, engagement and reach guidance in its professional dashboard, including personalized advice.

**Limit:** No evidence here for a universal posting time or hashtag count.

**Application:** Use account-specific guidance after launch and log changes.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y05 · Meta: how AI ranks content

[Source](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/how-ai-ranks-content-on-facebook-and-instagram/) · platform

Ranking combines signals and predictions about relevance and value.

**Limit:** Historical explanation; not a current numeric ranking recipe.

**Application:** Reject unverified claims that one interaction always has a fixed multiplier.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## Y06 · YouTube: deceptive practices policy

[Source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en) · platform

Misleading metadata and thumbnails that promise content absent from the video are prohibited.

**Limit:** Policy is a boundary, not a copywriting effectiveness study.

**Application:** Every title must be paid off by the exact narration and visual.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## B01 · Pew 2025: adult social media use

[Source](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/) · survey

A US adult survey documents broad YouTube use and strong Instagram use among younger adults.

**Limit:** Age bins do not isolate men 16–35; usage is not fitness intent or willingness to follow us.

**Application:** Use Shorts and Reels as plausible channels; validate actual audience fit.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## B02 · Pew 2025: teens, social media and AI chatbots

[Source](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/) · survey

A weighted survey of 1,458 US teens finds widespread YouTube use; boys report more YouTube and Reddit use than girls, but most boys do not report Reddit use.

**Limit:** US ages 13–17; not a map of all male beginners or their fitness search journeys.

**Application:** Use Reddit for question discovery, not as a representative audience sample.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## B03 · Ehrenberg-Bass: Brands of Distinction

[Source](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-of-distinction) · brand_framework

Distinctive assets depend on recognition and uniqueness; new elements require testing and consistent use.

**Limit:** A proposed mark or color has no established recognition yet.

**Application:** Keep the name plus a repeated rep-to-reason motif; test unaided recall and misattribution.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## B04 · W3C: contrast minimum

[Source](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) · design_standard

Text contrast guidance uses 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

**Limit:** A text contrast check is not complete accessibility certification for a video.

**Application:** Use 4.5:1 for all text pairs as our benchmark and test at phone size.

**Access:** Primary page or abstract reviewed, 2026-10-22

## E03 · Robinson et al. 2024 — proximity to failure

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/) · fitness_primary

Hypertrophy tended to increase as sets ended closer to failure; strength showed a different pattern.

**Limit:** Repetitions in reserve were estimated from study descriptions. Exact optimal proximity remains uncertain; this does not require every set to reach failure.

**Application:** Support the named episode claim, not all beginner advice.

**Access:** Primary abstract reviewed 2026-10-21 in V2; original record retained below.

## E06 · 2023 review — free weights versus machines

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37582807/) · fitness_primary

No muscle-growth difference was detected; strength transfer depended on the testing modality.

**Limit:** A non-significant average difference does not prove every exercise is interchangeable. No universal safety ranking.

**Application:** Support the named episode claim, not all beginner advice.

**Access:** Primary abstract reviewed 2026-10-21 in V2; original record retained below.

## E08 · Plotkin et al. 2022 — load versus repetition progression

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36199287/) · fitness_primary

Increasing repetitions and increasing load were both viable progression strategies in the studied lower-body program.

**Limit:** Short study in trained participants; not a promise that one extra rep measures muscle growth.

**Application:** Support the named episode claim, not all beginner advice.

**Access:** Primary abstract reviewed 2026-10-21 in V2; original record retained below.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27219125/) · fitness_primary

Early damage-related protein synthesis was not the same as the later response associated with hypertrophy.

**Limit:** Damage and soreness are not identical. This study alone does not validate a soreness threshold or a diagnostic test of growth.

**Application:** Support the named episode claim, not all beginner advice.

**Access:** Primary abstract reviewed 2026-10-21 in V2; original record retained below.

**Limit:** Broad adult evidence does not guarantee the same outcome for every two-day program or individual.

**Limit:** Small specific trial; not a universal rest-time prescription or evidence that longer is always better.
