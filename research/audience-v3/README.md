## Research scope and interpretation

The register contains 34 research/guidance records, 34 public community-question records and eight creator references. This is targeted English-language desk research, not a systematic review or representative survey. Some primary records were accessible only through indexed abstracts; each entry states access and scope. Creator research covers packaging and descriptions, not a complete visual or retention audit. Public questions are paraphrased without usernames or sensitive body details. Personas, journey pathways and most question-bank wording are original hypotheses.

Studies of news headlines, laboratory memory and live conversations support bounded design ideas; they do not supply expected Shorts growth. Scientific adult training evidence is not silently applied to minors. Numerical operating hurdles are project choices fixed before results, not platform benchmarks.

Technical media checks can validate files, words and layouts. Target-viewer understanding, brand preference, real reach, recurring operator labor and provider billing need separate observations. None has been invented. Nothing has been published or scheduled.
