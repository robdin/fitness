## Findings that changed the plan

- Retain the already-created DEX direction; test acquisition focus and explanation design before redesigning the brand.
- Free, adaptable program substitutes make a generic paid workout PDF a weak default hypothesis.
- Build an application and decision-support offer; prove the repeated job before choosing subscriptions.
- Separate randomized body screening, native package testing, and observational topic comparison.
- Keep reviewed recordings and deterministic diagrams as the initial production route; paid generation is optional and remains unconnected.
- A currently inaccessible private reference repository cannot be treated as audited or safely ported.

## Method and remaining retrieval limits

The expanded pass used first-party stores, official provider/platform guidance, original engineering research, a commercial subscription report, peer-reviewed records and an author preprint. Forum evidence remains a purposive sample; two prior threads were revisited in depth. No new representative survey, interviews, paid-community inspection or competitor revenue data were obtained. A consistent sample of thirty video listings across five channels is now recorded in [VIDEO_AUDIT.md](VIDEO_AUDIT.md); full-video/transcript coding and a smaller/inactive creator sample remain outstanding.

Several broad searches produced irrelevant pages; these were excluded. Exact titles and official links were more useful. Ovid returned a payment restriction, several publisher/Europe PMC pages were inaccessible, and PubMed sometimes returned no extractable body. Those failures are recorded as retrieval limits, not absence of evidence. The newly verified Burke source is its author preprint, not a claim of having read the later journal version.

No participant outreach, account signup, purchase, public publishing or paid generation was performed.
