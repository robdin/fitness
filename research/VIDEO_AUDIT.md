# YouTube listing audit — 30 observations

Observed 20 September 2026. Source data: [video_sample.json](video_sample.json). Each row retains its direct video URL, displayed age/views, listing duration and membership-only status.

## Sampling and scope

Take the first six visible items on each of five channel Videos pages. This is a consistent small listing sample, not a random or representative channel census. Established creators are overrepresented. Some displayed lists contain older videos. Do not infer why a channel's visible list is old or extrapolate current posting cadence from this sample.

The audit inspects titles, age/view labels and duration metadata. It does **not** claim full-video viewing, transcript coding, verification of health claims inside a title, private analytics or revenue data. Duration labels sometimes round to a whole minute; medians below inherit that precision.

## Editorial interpretation

- Trainer Winny's selected titles emphasize visible outcomes, rankings and mistakes. The roughly eight-to-fifteen-minute listing range is an observation, not evidence for a mandatory duration floor. Do not borrow an exaggerated promise without independent substantiation.
- PictureFit's selected explanations are much shorter and include a public correction theme. This supports considering a concise decision unit; it does not show that short videos cause better comprehension or revenue.
- House of Hypertrophy names studies, training comparisons and researcher disputes in longer listings. Research detail is already a visible competitive approach, so “science-based” alone is weak differentiation.
- Jeff Nippard's sample includes transformation stories, exercise selections and visual reference topics. Their scale and displayed views do not predict a new channel's acquisition.
- RP's page mixes public topics and members-only material. Three items lack public view counts; those values are **unknown**, never zero. The sample reveals a membership packaging route, not its conversion or retention.

These are researcher's interpretations of public metadata. Each underlying title is linked in the data file, and its promise is not treated as a scientific conclusion.

## Implications for DEX

1. Keep the initial screen focused on explanatory sequence; the observed length range does not identify an optimal runtime.
2. Test a specific decision-and-application promise against actual viewer needs.
3. Make caveats and corrections usable, not a generic credibility label.
4. Keep the final character design separate from this sequence test.
5. Do not normalize rounded age/view labels into a spurious performance score.

## Next depth pass

Add 10–20 smaller, inactive or less successful channels/videos using predeclared selection rules. Inspect full videos or lawfully available transcripts for opening promise, evidence use, visual burden, worked examples, caveats and CTA. Score ordinary and successful uploads within each channel over comparable ages and windows. Retain original summaries and short necessary excerpts rather than copied scripts.

No observed metadata here identifies a causal format winner. The current three pilot hypotheses come from the combined business question and evidence review, not a claim that competitor views proved them.
