# Customer research instruments

No interviews or participant outreach have been conducted. The current evidence is desk research and purposive public observation.

## Problem interviews

Target 12–15 adult recreational lifters with varied time/equipment constraints and experience. Record recruitment source and which perspectives are missing. Keep consent and contact details in controlled records outside Git. Ask permission separately before recording.

Start with behavior:
1. Tell me about the last session that did not fit your schedule or equipment.
2. What exactly happened, and what did you do?
3. What were you trying to preserve when you changed the plan?
4. Where did you look for help? Show the resource if you are comfortable.
5. What helped, what remained uncertain, and what did you do next?
6. Have you paid for help with this before? What was purchased and used?
7. How often has a similar situation occurred recently?
8. What would make this question unimportant?

Only then show a worksheet. Ask the person to use it on their own example, while avoiding unnecessary health details. Observe the unclear field, time to a useful output and whether it changes the next question. Do not lead with “Would you subscribe to an AI fitness tool?”

## Coding

Keep separate columns for observation, participant's explanation and researcher's inference. Code trigger, frequency, consequence, workaround, spend, source trust, unresolved question and resource behavior. Preserve counterexamples and “no problem” cases.

A compelling quotation does not establish prevalence. Do not select only respondents who admire the channel or already want the proposed product.

## Offer investigation

After the problem/resource work, present one concrete scoped offer. Ask what existing work it would replace and why someone would decline. Treat hypothetical willingness-to-pay as weak evidence. Actual paid delivery, activation and renewal require a later test.

## Forum synthesis

The source register contains a small purposive set of Reddit discussions about time constraints, program/app value and subscriptions. Two were revisited during the expanded pass. The directional themes inform interview questions; they are not a representative needs survey.

Do not copy usernames into customer lists, infer health profiles or send unsolicited promotion. Community rules and actual participation context determine whether a transparent research invitation is appropriate.

## Observation record

Use pseudonym, recruitment source, date, eligibility basis, consent record location, recent incident, workaround, resource attempt, uncertainty and next decision. Keep free text outside Git. De-identify synthesized findings and preserve the distinction between a research note and a measured business result.
