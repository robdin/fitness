## The positioning gap

The 16 current B-series videos teach the beginning of a gym/strength journey. B09 covers protein powder, while recovery and habit topics are mostly framed around gym sessions. There is no dedicated cardio, walking, sleep or recreational-sport episode. A broad brand name alone will not demonstrate a broad editorial promise.

Keep eleven useful existing episodes. Propose five replacements so the first month visibly introduces additional activities, everyday food decisions and sleep routines. Preserve four core videos per week and the current scheduling slots; no posts are scheduled by this proposal. The replaced videos remain reusable in the library.

## Three execution examples using the existing system

**Protein powder / B09:** an “optional add-on” graphic introduces the question, then a straightforward explanation separates convenience from a starting requirement. Keep the original claim scope and source treatment. The cultural wording changes the opening, not the evidence. Plain fallback: “Do you need protein powder to start?”

**First walk / P-MOVE-01:** a simple calendar opens to an available slot; a route card shows an explicitly illustrative walk. The action is planning, not hitting an invented health score. Plain fallback: “How would a walk fit into your week?” The script still needs its own researched guidance before production.

**Missed session / B16:** a crossed-out Tuesday turns into the next realistic calendar slot. Humor can live in the caption, but the spoken instruction remains clear and non-punitive. This is useful for any of the three proposed brands and does not need a trending sound.

## What the selected brand would change

Offscreen Athletic would emphasize the transition from explanation to an ordinary action. Minor Upgrades would emphasize one adjustment per episode. Active Lore would emphasize the question and why people are confused. Keep the same factual answer while testing these packages.

After a name is selected and screened, update the renderer's brand strings, covers, end cards, profile copy, source pages and delivery filenames together. Search for the previous working name and review each occurrence rather than replacing historical research indiscriminately. Recheck text fit, captions and uploader safe areas after any name change.

Five new topics exceed a cosmetic rebrand. Estimate their research, narration and rendering effort before accepting the replacements. If they cannot fit the launch capacity, use four balanced weeks assembled from the ready library plus only the new episodes that pass review; do not silently label a strength-only launch as validation of broad fitness demand.
