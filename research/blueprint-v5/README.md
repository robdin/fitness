## Checks and unresolved learning

Every new script uses six beats, an early answer and one useful action. Guidance is labeled guidance. Sources do not imply that the brand's complete method was clinically tested. The broader vision calls for inclusive adult validation; existing adult-male studies retain their actual populations.

The source-label and motion pairs have separate protocols/stimulus records. The month retains eight question-led and eight answer-led openings balanced by week and weekday; the new topic mix is not balanced by pillar. Do not report a causal hook effect from organic results.

Technical production checks are recorded in the [production handoff](../../docs/MONTH_ONE_V5.md). Source review, frame tests, ASR and successful rendering do not establish audience interest. Real comprehension checks, platform observations and recurring human labor remain required for the go/no-go decision.
