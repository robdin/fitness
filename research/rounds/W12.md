# W12 — Economics and finance

Research round A/C · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Measure cash and human time separately; model prices as scenarios.

## Evidence and interpretation

Credits are not currency, annual bills are not monthly cash flows, and one-time sales are not MRR. Existing subscriptions may lower incremental cash costs but still have opportunity cost. Model paid generation, failures, expert review, refunds, fees and support.

Sources: [W02: RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [T01: OpenArt MCP & CLI](https://openart.ai/mcp/); [T02: Higgsfield API](https://docs.higgsfield.ai/docs); [T07: Stripe Payment Links](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links)

## Alternatives

Low-cash manual pilot; additional software subscriptions; contractor-supported production.

## Experiment and decision gate

Use the economics script with explicit assumptions, then replace them with three actual runs. A positive margin before labor is not sufficient evidence of owner income.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Founder/analyst. Still needed: Budget, actual fees, taxes, labor rates and measured fulfillment.
