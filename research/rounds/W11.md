# W11 — Customer success and retention

Research round E · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Define activation as completing a useful decision, and recurrence as another relevant use.

## Evidence and interpretation

Commercial app benchmarks have different acquisition and fulfillment conditions. Borrow clear definitions, not retention forecasts. Separate voluntary cancellation, payment failure and a customer who simply finished the job.

Sources: [W02: RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [M03: RP Hypertrophy App](https://rpstrength.com/pages/hypertrophy-app); [M04: MASS Research Review](https://massresearchreview.com/); [M09: Hypertrophy Coach](https://hypertrophycoach.com/)

## Alternatives

Self-serve resource; bounded group support; individual service.

## Experiment and decision gate

Observe onboarding, record support minutes, then follow two renewal opportunities if a recurring pilot is justified. Preserve actual cohort denominators.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Customer success owner. Still needed: No customer or renewal data.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
