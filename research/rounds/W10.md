# W10 — Product and monetization

Research round B · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Test a guided decision toolkit; earn a subscription through repeat use.

## Evidence and interpretation

Programs, research subscriptions, app utility, and coaching are different products. A worksheet pack alone faces strong free substitutes. The proposed paid toolkit adds worked examples and bounded learning support; its value and price are unvalidated.

Sources: [M01: Bodybuilding Simplified](https://bodybuildingsimplified.com/); [M03: RP Hypertrophy App](https://rpstrength.com/pages/hypertrophy-app); [M04: MASS Research Review](https://massresearchreview.com/); [M07: Stronger by Science program bundle](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/program-bundle/); [M09: Hypertrophy Coach](https://hypertrophycoach.com/); [T07: Stripe Payment Links](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links)

## Alternatives

One-time guided product; recurring maintained utility; high-touch coaching.

## Experiment and decision gate

Show the actual toolkit before taking payment. Track purchase, use, refunds, support and a later request for ongoing help. Price only after scope and review costs are clear.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Founder. Still needed: No checkout, sales or repeat-use cohort.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
