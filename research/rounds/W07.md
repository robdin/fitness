# W07 — Creative production

Research round C · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Use deterministic cards for the first sequence test and preserve recording-first final production.

## Evidence and interpretation

Six silent prototypes share original text, palette, and cards; they are timing/layout studies. Accepted narration will determine final duration. A mascot rig and generated motion can be evaluated after the explanation works. More scene changes are not a quality metric.

Sources: [E01: Guo, Kim & Rubin, 2014](https://pure.kaist.ac.kr/en/publications/how-video-production-affects-student-engagement-an-empirical-stud/); [E02: Murphy et al., 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/acp.3899); [T01: OpenArt MCP & CLI](https://openart.ai/mcp/); [T02: Higgsfield API](https://docs.higgsfield.ai/docs)

## Alternatives

Cards with narration; reusable character rig; selective generated motion.

## Experiment and decision gate

Measure render time, editing time, recording time, accepted assets and rework. Test readable cards on a phone before polishing motion.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Producer. Still needed: Human narration, final rig, visual review, provider-account benchmarks.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
