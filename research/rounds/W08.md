# W08 — GTM and distribution

Research round C · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Use YouTube for discovery, a relevant free resource for application, and email for a continuing relationship.

## Evidence and interpretation

Native title/thumbnail tests are available for eligible content. Different video bodies are not randomized by simply uploading both. Shorts require their own discovery and destination checks; preserve their caveats.

Sources: [P01: Native A/B tests](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [P08: Sharing links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en); [P09: Related video on Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en); [Y04: Jeff Nippard](https://www.youtube.com/@JeffNippard)

## Alternatives

Long-form first; Shorts discovery; partnerships after evidence of audience fit.

## Experiment and decision gate

Screen A/B bodies with randomized participants, publish one selected body per topic, then use native packaging tests. Compare topic uploads observationally.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Founder/growth. Still needed: No channel access, public release, native tests or acquisition results yet.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
