# W01 — Business thesis and constraints

Research round A · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Build a useful education-and-application business; keep subscriptions optional.

## Evidence and interpretation

A content-only business has simpler fulfillment; coaching has stronger personalization but more human work. The current prototypes use no paid generation. Income targets, weekly capacity, reviewer budget, and acceptable loss remain founder inputs.

Sources: [W02: RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [M07: Stronger by Science program bundle](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/program-bundle/); [M09: Hypertrophy Coach](https://hypertrophycoach.com/)

## Alternatives

Revenue-first coaching; ads-only media; an educational product ladder.

## Experiment and decision gate

Use a four-week capacity ledger before committing to cadence. Stop expansion if required review does not fit available time.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Founder. Still needed: Founder hours, maximum loss, desired income, commercial scope.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
