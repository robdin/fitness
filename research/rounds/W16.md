# W16 — Data and measurement

Research round D · 20 September 2026 · Desk pass completed; operating validation pending.

## Decision

Give every observation a unit, denominator, period and source.

## Evidence and interpretation

The experiment store preserves assignments, responses and missingness. Native packaging results are separate from body tests. Payment events are deduplicated but not blindly summed; checkout completion and invoice payment can describe the same customer journey.

Sources: [P01: Native A/B tests](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [P05: Analytics metrics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics); [P06: Reporting metrics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/reporting/v1/reports/metrics); [T05: Sample ratio mismatch in controlled experiments](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/diagnosing-sample-ratio-mismatch-in-online-controlled-experiments-a-taxonomy-and-rules-of-thumb-for-practitioners/); [T08: Stripe webhook documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks)

## Alternatives

Manual reconciled exports; local event store; analytics service later.

## Experiment and decision gate

Follow a test participant and test customer from assignment through response or payment. Verify that duplicate events do not double-count and missing metrics remain null.

## Ownership and limits

Owner: Analytics owner. Still needed: Real Studio exports, customer events, attribution and revenue reconciliation.

This recommendation is provisional. A source-backed observation is not proof of customer demand or a passed operating gate. Record the result in the decision log; do not silently promote this desk pass to validated status.
