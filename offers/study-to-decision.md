A citation is a starting point for checking a claim. It does not establish that every adjacent recommendation applies to you. A review's total study count may differ from the evidence count for a particular outcome.

Do not invent missing methods or numerical results from an abstract. Leave unknowns explicit.

Feedback: Was the outcome clear? Did the card prevent an unjustified conclusion?
