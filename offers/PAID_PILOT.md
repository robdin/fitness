# Guided decision toolkit — proposed paid pilot

Status: concept and scope only. No checkout, sale, delivery or recurring commitment exists.

## Customer promise

Help an adult recreational lifter document a practical training question, understand a worked evidence example and prepare a more specific next decision. Do not promise individualized programming, injury treatment, guaranteed physique changes or personal medical advice.

## Bounded deliverables

- The three revised worksheets, after viewer feedback.
- Six original worked examples with source notes and limits.
- One recorded walkthrough and one scheduled group application session.
- A short guide to asking a coach or other appropriate professional a concrete question.
- Corrections and access terms stated for the actual purchased version.

The worksheets are drafted. The six paid examples, walkthrough, group session and delivery system have not been produced.

## Commercial test

Start with a clearly described one-time offer after useful free-resource behavior is observed. An earlier $29 planning price remains an untested hypothesis. Cap the first delivery cohort at ten customers to measure support and fulfillment. Specify dates, timezone, inclusions, exclusions, refund process and contact route before opening checkout.

Do not accept payment for an unspecified future product. Do not count survey interest as demand. Record actual paid amount, payment status, activation, support time, refund and the problem the product replaced.

## Recurring option, only if supported

A possible membership would maintain a reviewed case library, answer recurring application questions within a defined scope and provide a scheduled educational session. Earlier $19/month arithmetic is not a chosen offer. Customers must understand what is delivered each period, how cancellation works and when access ends.

Test repeat use first. If most value comes from one download, keep the one-time product. A community needs moderation and a real reason to return; it is not an automatic retention feature.

## Ready-to-sell checklist

Named business/sender; deliverables complete or accurately described with dates; relevant reviewer; actual jurisdiction and payment account checked; checkout terms; reliable access and receipts; support owner; refund/cancellation path; privacy/retention decision; end-to-end test including a failed payment.

These are unresolved operating requirements, not claims of legal or professional clearance.
