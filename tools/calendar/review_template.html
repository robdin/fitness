Test the idea in three stages

1

Can people follow it?

Use the existing matched A/B/C visual exports for a 12–18-person usability screen. Check five narrated V2 clips with at least ten distinct target adults. Record unaided understanding and critical errors.

2

Will people stay?

Eight question-led and eight answer-led openings, balanced across weeks, topics and weekdays. Keep voice, style and duration stable. Organic differences are descriptive; this is not randomized audience assignment.

3

Can we sustain it?

Log research, editing, review, corrections, posting and analytics. The weekly ceiling is 12 hands-on hours. Allow one documented process simplification; unattended rendering is recorded separately.

Proposed decision hurdles

  • 12 mature releases; at least 8 with 100 engaged YouTube views.
  • Median stayed-to-watch ≥60%; median percentage viewed ≥70%.
  • 5 distinct useful audience responses.
  • ≥80% comprehension among at least 10 V2 target viewers.
  • Last two measured production weeks ≤12 hours each.
Operating choices set before launch—not industry benchmarks or statistical proof.

What the evidence means

Go: all gates pass and recurring critical misunderstandings are resolved. Commit to one bounded second month.

Iterate: mixed evidence or a repairable clarity/process issue.

No-go: adequate evidence shows both attention and usefulness fail, or the process still exceeds capacity after one simplification.

Insufficient: reach or required records are missing. One 14-day extension; no invented zeroes.
