# Experiment and business analysis task

Input: frozen protocol, actual assignment/exposure/response records, metric definitions, source windows, costs and known missingness.

Check the unit of randomization, sample ratios, duplicate people, missing observations, protocol deviations and overlapping platform snapshots before interpreting results. Analyze each declared comparison. Separate exploratory patterns from confirmatory inference. A better title is not evidence of a true health claim; a sale is not renewal.

Output: observed_facts, quality_issues, descriptive_results, uncertainty, alternative_explanations, decision_options and next_measurement. Never insert synthetic observations into real reports, average CTR without valid denominators, or select a winner from noisy early results.
