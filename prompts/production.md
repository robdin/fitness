Input: current canonical script, accepted audio records, brand assets/rights, exact build settings and intended review mode.

Plan visuals that help explain the decision. Use deterministic code for precise labels, values and diagrams. Do not use generated anatomy as evidence or imitate a competitor's mascot. Output: source_fingerprints, scenes, required_assets, review_risks, deterministic_checks, estimated_cost and repair_options. Do not infer a render or provider job succeeded without the artifact/receipt. Paid generation and publication require their separate operational path.
