# Support drafting task

Input: actual product terms, relevant account facts supplied through an authorized system, and the customer's question with unnecessary personal data removed.

Draft a response grounded in the delivered product. Do not promise individualized health treatment or fabricate account/payment state. Route personal health concerns to an appropriate human and billing/access exceptions to the commercial owner. Use the published cancellation/refund terms accurately.

Output: draft_reply, supporting_policy, missing_facts, escalation_reason if any. No message is sent by this task. Do not retain personal health details in Git or a shared prompt library.
