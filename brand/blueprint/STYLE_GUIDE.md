# Blueprint · selected production style

Selected by the owner on 23 October 2026. This replaces Training Club as the active style for the new [Blueprint month](../../content/month-one-v5/CALENDAR.md). Daylight and Afterglow remain historical alternatives. **Blueprint is the style name; the public brand name is undecided.** Use “Body of our dreams” as campaign wording, not a claim that a name has been cleared.

## Promise and voice

**Your dream body. Your real life.** Personal aesthetics, health and capability belong alongside work, relationships, study, enjoyment and recovery. The origin is the owner's friend saying she wanted the “body of her dreams” despite a busy life. The first carousel tells that story without inventing a transformation or testimonial.

Mission: help people work toward the body, health and confidence they want through science-backed training, movement and nutrition that fit everyday life. Vision: caring for your body is woven into how you live.

Keep the voice specific, warm and occasionally amused by ordinary friction. Science supports the named claim; 80/20 means useful priorities, not a universal results ratio. Give an early answer, a visible example, a boundary and one next step. Aesthetic goals are self-defined; avoid one ideal body, guaranteed transformations and deadline pressure.

## Visual rules

- Cobalt `#2348C6` is the main video surface. White `#FFFFFF` carries the primary explanation.
- Paper `#F3F5F4` holds diagrams and captions; ink `#173249` carries their content.
- Lilac `#DDD6F5` identifies the current step. Cloud `#DCEAF5` carries secondary labels on cobalt.
- Fine grids provide structure behind diagrams. A soft light field sits below essential content. Neither replaces a meaningful explanation.
- Fraunces Italic appears only in the first short headline and supporting-post covers. Space Grotesk carries the rest, including all captions, numbers, qualifications and source labels.
- No AI character, portrait slot, fabricated body score or study-like chart made from illustrative numbers.

The canonical production values are [tokens.json](tokens.json). The Fraunces font comes from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces), downloaded 23 October 2026; its unmodified font and SIL OFL license are retained under `fonts`. Space Grotesk and its license remain in `content/mvp/time-audit/fonts`. File hashes are bound into every video timeline.

## Content components

1. Campaign wordmark and series label.
2. A short, specific headline, with no introductory logo animation.
3. One explanatory object: checklist, clock, route, food example, log or calendar.
4. A visible limitation and a claim-specific source cue. Editorial advice is labeled editorial.
5. Full synchronized captions and a small campaign sign-off.

Series: **Dream / Plan** connects ambition to a usable week; **Worth your effort** explains priorities and evidence; **Life happens** addresses setbacks and constraints. The episode topic does the work; not every narration repeats the campaign line.

## Production and accessibility

Vertical masters: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC stereo. Keep critical text within x=75–925/y=240–1500. Captions use 43 px or larger, no more than two lines, and at most 20 characters/second. These are house margins; actual uploader overlays still require review. Preserve the approved voice's speed and pitch.

Carousels: 1080 × 1350, five panels each. Stories: 1080 × 1920, two frames each. Prompt frames reserve x=112/y=1010/w=762/h=205 for a real native Instagram poll. The image itself does not collect answers. Posting-copy files specify the poll options.

Source cues remain readable on a solid background. Complete references and material qualifications also accompany the posting copy. Public guidance and editorial examples are distinguished from trials. Supporting posts inherit the applicable claim boundaries; the founder story is attributed to the owner.
