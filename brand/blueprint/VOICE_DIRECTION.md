## What to sound like

A friendly, informed peer explaining something useful to one person. Warm, relaxed, conversational. Everyday words, contractions, natural pauses, gentle emphasis and occasional humor rooted in the situation. Avoid formal lecture wording, a booming announcer, forced slang, invented personal experience and constant motivational energy. Keep evidence and material qualifications precise.

The existing private speaker profile is used for the first delivery audition. This is a change in performance, not a new speaker casting or a claim that the voice is already audience-approved. The earlier instruction to preserve the neutral delivery is superseded for new work. Existing masters still contain that earlier audio.
